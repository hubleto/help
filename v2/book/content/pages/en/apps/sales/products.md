# Products

The Products section is the catalog of goods and services offered by your company. It keeps product classification, pricing, physical details, packaging, images, and supplier information in one place for use across sales and order workflows.

## Key capabilities

* **Product types:** Classify an item as Consumable, Storable, or Service so that it can be handled appropriately in related workflows.
* **Pricing and margin:** Set the sales price, margin, VAT, invoicing policy, and whether the price is shown to customers.
* **Units and physical details:** Define the base unit, dimensions, weight, packaging, and lot or batch tracking.
* **Supplier management:** Link suppliers and store supplier-specific names, codes, purchase prices, delivery times, and notes.
* **Catalog content:** Maintain EAN, name, group, category, description, internal notes, and product images.
* **Sales integration:** Reuse product information in deals, quotes, orders, and other connected sales processes.
