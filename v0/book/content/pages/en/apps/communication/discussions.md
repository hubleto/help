# Discussions

Discussions provide a dedicated space for your team to communicate, share ideas, and collaborate within Hubleto. Each discussion focuses on a specific topic, helping participants understand the context before they read or add new messages.

Keeping these conversations in Hubleto creates a shared and searchable record of communication. Important questions, feedback, and decisions are less likely to get lost in long email threads, and colleagues can return to an earlier conversation when they need to understand why a decision was made.

## When to use Discussions

Create a discussion whenever a topic is likely to involve several messages or should remain available for future reference. For example, a team can use a discussion to collect suggestions for an internal process, review an idea before implementation, coordinate work on a customer request, or record the decisions made during a project.

Short questions can also be handled in Discussions when the answer may be useful to other colleagues later. This gradually creates a practical history of the team's knowledge and decisions.

## Key features

* **Create and name discussions:** You can start a discussion about any topic and give it a clear, descriptive title. A specific title helps teammates recognize the subject without opening every conversation.
* **Add descriptions and notes:** A description can explain the purpose and expected outcome of the discussion, while notes can preserve additional context that should remain easy to find.
* **Assign a main moderator:** Each discussion identifies a main moderator who is responsible for guiding the conversation and keeping its purpose clear.
* **Exchange messages:** Participants can ask questions, share ideas, gather feedback, and maintain a continuous record of decisions. Messages support Markdown, allowing basic formatting such as headings, lists, emphasis, and links.
* **Search and browse:** The discussion list can be searched and filtered, making it easier to find an existing topic or review a previous conversation.
* **Close completed discussions:** A discussion can be marked as closed when the topic has been resolved or no further replies are expected. The existing conversation remains available for reference.

## Organizing a discussion

Use the title to summarize the subject in a few words and the description to explain what needs to be discussed. If the conversation should lead to a decision, state the question or expected outcome clearly in the description. This gives every participant the same starting point and reduces unnecessary clarification.

Messages then form the chronological conversation around that topic. Because all replies remain together, a teammate joining later can read the existing messages before contributing. Notes can be used for supporting information that does not belong in the main message flow.

## Simple collaboration

Whether you need to ask a question, gather feedback, or maintain a record of decisions, Discussions keep team communication organized and easy to access. A consistent naming style and one clearly defined topic per discussion will also make searches more useful as the number of conversations grows.

Before opening a new discussion, search for the topic to see whether a suitable conversation already exists. When a topic has been resolved, summarize the result in a final message and close the discussion. This leaves future readers with both the conversation and a clear outcome.
