The Communication section brings team conversations together inside Hubleto. It gives colleagues a shared place to exchange information without separating the conversation from the system in which their work is already managed.

Instead of relying only on email or disconnected messaging tools, your team can keep a searchable history of questions, ideas, feedback, and decisions. This makes it easier for other team members to understand what has already been discussed and continue the conversation with the necessary context.

## Available app

The **Discussions** app is the main collaboration space in this section. Use it to create topic-based conversations, exchange messages, and return to earlier discussions whenever information needs to be reviewed.

Communication in Hubleto is especially useful for conversations that should remain available as part of the team's shared working knowledge rather than disappearing in personal inboxes.
