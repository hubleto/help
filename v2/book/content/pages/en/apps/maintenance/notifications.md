# Notifications

Notifications bring important updates from Hubleto into one place. They can inform a user about activity in the system, such as a change to a record, without requiring the user to search through every app. The inbox keeps received notifications together so that you can review them when they are relevant to your work.

Unlike a general-purpose chat or email app, Notifications primarily displays messages generated by Hubleto and its applications. Notifications are addressed to individual users; the current inbox does not provide a standard compose form for broadcasting a message to an entire team or role.

## What a notification contains

Each notification stores the information needed to identify its source and understand why it was sent:

* **Recipient and sender:** The *To* and *From* fields identify the user receiving the notification and the user associated with sending it.
* **Subject and body:** A short subject summarizes the update, while the body provides its details and context.
* **Category and priority:** These fields help distinguish different kinds of notifications. Available category names depend on the applications installed and configured in your Hubleto instance.
* **Related link or record:** Some notifications include a URL or a reference to the record that triggered them, allowing you to open the relevant context directly.
* **Sent and read times:** Hubleto records when a notification was sent and, where applicable, when it was marked as read.

## Managing your inbox

Unread notifications are highlighted in the list. After reviewing one, you can mark it as read; if you need to return to it later, you can mark it as unread again. You can also search the list to find earlier updates. This makes Notifications a useful place to keep track of changes and alerts that might otherwise be overlooked.
