The Productivity section is used to manage projects, tasks, work logs, and issues.

Here you'll find:

  * **Tasks** - manage individual assignments.
  * **Worksheets** - record worked hours.
  * **Projects** - manage projects, budgets, and phases.
  * **Issues** - track problems and errors during a project.

As in other parts of Hubleto, you can use the **funnel icon** (filter) and **gear icon** (column settings) to customize your view.
